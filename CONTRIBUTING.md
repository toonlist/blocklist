## Contribution Rules

This repository is **primarily for Toontown Rewritten** and exists to help players make informed decisions about who they choose to block.

### Scope

* Entries **must relate to Toontown Rewritten**.
* Do **not** submit players from other games or platforms.
* This list is informational only and does **not** represent official moderation action.

### What Can Be Submitted

You may submit a player **only if** they consistently exhibit one or more of the following:

* Clearly **non-genuine / automated behavior**
* Actions that intentionally ruin another player’s experience (beyond normal gameplay interactions).
* Repeated harassment, bullying, or disrespectful chat that goes against the spirit of “Respect Other Toons” and “Take It Easy.”
* Behavior widely observed by multiple players

### Evidence Requirements

* Submissions **must include evidence**, such as:

  * Screenshots
  * Video clips
* “Trust me” reports will be rejected.

### What Is NOT Allowed

* No witch hunts or harassment
* No personal attacks, insults, or slurs
* No speculation or assumptions about intent
* No doxxing or personal information
* No reports based on:

  * Single disagreements
  * Playstyle differences
  * One-time mistakes or lag issues

### Naming & Tone

* Be factual and neutral.
* Describe **observable behavior only**.
* Do **not** state claims as absolute truth.
* Avoid emotional or inflammatory language.

### Removal & Disputes

* Entries may be removed if:

  * Evidence is insufficient
  * Behavior changes over time
  * The entry is outdated or inaccurate
* Players may request review or removal via an issue, with justification.

### Disclaimer

This list:

* Is **community-maintained**
* Is **not affiliated with Toontown Rewritten staff**
* Exists to help players decide who *they personally* wish to block

Abuse of this repository will result in rejected contributions or bans.
